---
title: Authentication
description: Google sign-in in the free version, magic link and email in Pro.
---

There is no password anywhere in this starter. The free version signs people in with Google: set `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID` and `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET` (see [Google OAuth](/docs/google-oauth)) and the button appears on `/login` and `/register`.

## Email sign-in

Magic-link sign-in and the transactional emails behind it (invitations, receipts, delivery log) ship with Pro. The email field on the login page is a disabled preview, and the free server registers no email sign-in endpoint, so it can't be switched on from the browser.
